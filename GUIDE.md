# What The Ground Keeps: player guide

The same guide is in the game under **How to play** on the main menu and the pause menu.

## Controls

| Action | Key |
|---|---|
| Move / look | WASD / mouse |
| Run, crouch, jump | Shift, Ctrl or C, Space |
| Interact | **E**. Look at something until a prompt appears at the centre of the screen. If the prompt says **Hold**, keep E pressed. |
| Flashlight | F |
| Pour fuel (last chapter) | Hold the left mouse button |
| Tonight's task list | Hold Tab |
| Pause (settings, save, quit) | Esc |

## How to make progress

The **objective** in the top-left corner always tells you what to do next. The game saves at
the start of every chapter, and **Continue** resumes from there.

It is always night: the story takes place over one night of dying and three night shifts.
There is no day/night cycle. The only dawn comes at the very end.

1. **Prologue:** a cutscene of the drive and the crash. Watch it. Esc skips where that is allowed.
2. **Awakening:** follow the blood trail south along the road to the metal hatch at the gas
   station. Then go into the store and **look at Mr. Grethnar behind the counter and press E**.
   Choose "Fine. Three nights."
3. **Night One:** hold Tab to see the tasks.
   - Pump fuel for the customer: take the nozzle at the pump named in the hint, then hold E.
     Stopping at exactly the amount asked for earns a tip.
   - Restock two shelves: the boxes are in the storage room. Carry one to a shelf and press E.
   - Mop the stains: take the mop from the bucket, look at a stain and hold E.
   - Take the trash bag out to the dumpster behind the store.

   When every task is done, **go back to Grethnar and press E**. He gives you a clue, and the
   next night starts. If you talk to him before you're finished, he tells you what is left.
4. **Night Two:**
   - Pump fuel for a customer.
   - Ring up the walk-in customer at the register.
   - Dip the tank at the hatch.
   - Fetch the **ledger** from the office of **Blackwood College**, the large brick building
     across the road. Then report to Grethnar.
5. **Night Three:**
   - Mop the stains.
   - When the power dies, reset the breakers in the storage room.
   - Report to Grethnar. He sends you to relight the **boiler** in the college basement: the
     stairs are at the back right of the ground floor.
   - After the boiler, push the bookshelf aside and talk to the old man.
6. **The Key:**
   - Go to the cow field south-east of the station and take the **shovel** from the barn at
     the far side.
   - Count the cow skeletons starting from the fence by the road, and **hold E beside the
     third one** to dig.
   - Then **run** back over the fence.
   - Use the key on the iron door in the hidden room of the college basement.
7. **Below:**
   - Go down the stairs and follow the torches to the far end to find Zain.
   - Don't touch the bodies on the ground.
   - Afterwards, run back to the stairs.
   - Shining the flashlight on the creatures scares them away. Three hits send you back to a
     checkpoint.
8. **Burn:**
   - Take the red fuel can beside the store and fill it at a pump (hold E).
   - Pour it (hold the left mouse button) near the **hatch**, the **pumps** and the **store**.
     You can refill the can.
   - Stand on the trail and press E to strike a match.
   - When Zain runs, reach him.
9. **Dawn:** the ending, then the credits.

## Settings (main menu or Esc, then Settings)

- **Graphics quality:** low, medium or high. If the game stutters, start with **low**.
- **Render scale:** lower is faster.
- **Volumetric fog** and **shadows:** turning these off gives the biggest speed-up.
- **FPS limit:** 30, 60, 90, 120, 144, 165, 240 or unlimited. **Show fps** puts a counter in the corner.
- **Draw distance:** 60–320 m. Nothing farther away is drawn, and only what is in view is ever drawn.
- **Grass density** and **vsync**. Turn vsync off to go above your monitor's refresh rate.
- **Movement speed:** 60–200%.
- **Field of view** and **mouse sensitivity**.
- **Invert look**, **head bob**, **brightness**, **spirit sight** (the ASCII effect), and
  **subtitles** and their size.
- **Master volume** and **fullscreen**. F11 also toggles fullscreen.
